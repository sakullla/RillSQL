Use case: logo-brand

Style/medium: premium minimal software identity, crisp geometric flat shapes with a very restrained two-tone silver highlight. The cobalt tile can have an extremely subtle top-left-to-bottom-right blue tonal shift, never glossy. Front-facing orthographic graphic, not a mockup or a perspective object.

Text: only the single custom geometric R emblem. No SQL text, no app name, no slogans.
